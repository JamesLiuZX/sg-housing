# Test against real listings

Nine flats on sale on 18 November 2026, pulled from the HDB Flat Portal, EdgeProp and Ohmyhome, each with its floor plan and five or six photos. The pictures are kept outside this repository: the portals' photos and HDB's floor plans are copyright and are used here only to judge our own output.

The question the test answers: when a buyer types the address of a flat they are about to view, is what Blk shows them true and does it look like a serious product?

## What the test shows

**The block is right for all nine.** Address, footprint, storeys, year and past sales come from official records, so the block stage is true for any of them.

**The layout is right for two or three of nine.** Blk carries three layouts, all recent BTO types. Listing 1 is the best case: its real plan is 12.4 m by 8.5 m with bedrooms of 3.2, 2.9 and 2.9 m along the facade and a 3.45 m living room; our 4-room layout is 12.5 m by 7.9 m with 3.4, 2.9, 2.9 and 3.3 m. Listings 2 and 8 are close cousins. The other six are flats Blk cannot draw at all today: a 1976 corridor flat, a 1984 New Generation flat, a 1997 5-room, a two-storey maisonette, a 47-storey premium flat and a jumbo. That is most of the resale market, since older flats are most of what is sold.

**Renovation changes more than we assumed.** One flat has its kitchen wall replaced by a glass partition; one has a study sold as a fourth bedroom; the maisonette's plan is titled "after renovation and reconfiguration". A typical layout is a starting point, and the product has to say so and let the person correct it.

**Facts on listings disagree with each other.** One listing says 103 sqm in the text and 93 in the field; another says "high floor" for storeys 6 to 10; a balcony is in the photos and "No" in the form. Blk's sourced facts are worth showing next to a listing's claims.

**Photos are often not the real room.** Four of the listings looked at carry notes such as "photos edited and decluttered", "Decluttering applied to photos via AI" and "Photos Virtually Staged". This is the gap Blk exists to fill: a model that is honest about being a model.

**What a real flat looks like, that our model did not.** Polished white floor tiles that mirror the windows; white walls with a skirting line; grey-tinted windows with grilles; wall-mounted air-conditioners; ceiling lights; built-in wardrobes; kitchens with dark worktops; rooms photographed level, with upright verticals and bright windows. The model does not need to copy the furniture. It needs these constants.

## What follows

1. **Layouts from real flat types.** Draw our own plans for the types these listings stand for (3-room Improved corridor, 4-room New Generation, 5-room Improved, 4-room premium point block, 3-room point block, Type-S) so six of nine become eight of nine. The maisonette needs a second storey in the engine and waits.
2. **Your own floor plan.** The only way to be exactly right for one flat is that flat's plan. Owners and buyers hold it; the upload-and-trace feature moves up the roadmap.
3. **The look.** Light that settles into soft shadow, real finishes (reflective tile, timber-look vinyl, skirting, HDB windows), the official map under the block, and blocks drawn as HDB blocks. In progress.
4. **Say what is known.** Each screen states which parts are from records (the block) and which are typical (the layout).

## Rules that bind us

- **HDB floor plans are copyright.** Each carries "No part of this floorplan may be reproduced or transmitted in any form or by any means." Blk never ships or displays HDB's drawings. Our layouts are our own drawings of typical flat types, and an uploaded plan stays on the person's device. A licence or a data-sharing arrangement with HDB is a question for the conversation with government.
- **Portals forbid automated collection.** EdgeProp's terms forbid crawling by any method; Ohmyhome's forbid harvesting. HDB's portal has no such clause but its content is copyright. So "paste a listing link" must not fetch the portal's page. It can read the address in the link or the pasted text, and take everything else from official records.
- **Listings carry personal data.** Portal responses include agents' names, phone numbers and registration numbers. None of it was kept, and Blk has no reason to hold it.
